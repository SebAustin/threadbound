# Video script (2:45)

Voice-over (VO) is optional. On-screen text works on its own. Plain words,
real footage only.

**0:00–0:10 · Cold open (`cold-open`)**
On screen: a hand pinches the chute; marbles bounce off a thread into a cup; a melody plays.
VO: *"Pinch a thread. Drop the marbles. Hear your solution."*

**0:10–0:30 · Learn it in seconds (`onboarding`)**
On screen: the ghost hand pinches a glowing peg and pulls; the player copies it.
Text: *"No menus. No controllers. Just pinch, pull, release."*
VO: *"Threadbound teaches itself. A ghost hand shows the move, and your first thread twangs into place."*

**0:30–0:50 · Every thread is a string (`three-cups`)**
On screen: drop; amber and azure marbles sort into their cups.
VO: *"Each thread is tuned by its length, so every bounce is a note and every solution is a song. Sort colors, and each one carries its own shape."*

**0:50–1:10 · Four worlds (`hinge`)**
On screen: pinch a rail tab; slide; the threads reshape; solved.
VO: *"Twenty-four puzzles in four worlds: trampolines, color sorting, sliding rails…"*

**1:10–1:25 · …and Tension (`spool`)**
On screen: a long thread is refused; the plaque explains.
VO: *"…and Tension, where your spool of thread runs short."*

**1:25–1:45 · Your melody book (`melody-book`)**
On screen: poke the stars; the tune replays.
VO: *"Solved levels keep their tune. Poke the stars to hear it again."*

**1:45–2:00 · A reason to come back (`daily`)**
On screen: the sun opens the daily; the streak on the plaque.
VO: *"A new daily puzzle every day, and a streak to keep."*

**2:00–2:35 · Made for everyone (`settings`, then gaze)**
On screen: Slow-mo on; Raise; then look and pinch with eye tracking.
VO: *"Play seated, one-handed, in slow motion, at your height. Colors always come with shapes. On eye-tracked headsets, just look and pinch."*

**2:35–2:45 · End card (`study`)**
On screen: the virtual study; then the card "Threadbound · sebaustin.github.io/threadbound · Play in Quest Browser".
VO: *"Threadbound. Play it now in Quest Browser."*
