# The room places the board; it doesn't shape the puzzle

The brief asks for purposeful passthrough: the real room should change the experience. Without a headset, nothing that makes the room part of the puzzle can be tuned or proven. That rules out cups on the real table, marbles spilling onto it, and persistent anchors, because IWER's rooms are grey stand-ins that can't show what an anchor adds. So the room's role is placement on a detected table, made robust (keep searching while the level is idle, place again after a recenter, fade in at the final pose), plus positional audio. The copy says exactly that and no more. Persistent anchors stay future work, and the unused `anchors`, `meshDetection` and `grabbing` features are switched off.

**Considered Options:** a tabletop marble spill you could film; puzzle cups on the real table (L effort); anchor-driven relocation.

**Consequences:** Experience Design concedes part of the passthrough line. Revisit if a real device becomes available (see 0002).
