# A thread's notes climb per bounce

Pitch used to come from a thread's length alone, so 17 of the 24 solutions played one repeated note, which undercut "every solution is a melody". Now a thread's length sets only its root. Each further bounce on that thread during a drop climbs the pentatonic scale, turning back down at the top. All of a drop's marbles share the climb, and it restarts at the root on every drop. A solve closes on a cadence from the cups. Notes are decoupled from physics, so restitution, the level proofs and "shorter rings higher" stay as they were.

**Considered Options:** widening the length-to-pitch range (it can't vary a single-thread level); a "fretboard" where the landing point picks the note (it ties music to landing positions a few millimetres apart); fixing it only in the video (dishonest).

**Consequences:** the same solution always plays the same tune, so the melody book stays meaningful, and its entries now keep their timing. Melody capture still samples once per rendered frame, so scoring a melody (a "song star") stays out until capture is proven frame-rate independent.
